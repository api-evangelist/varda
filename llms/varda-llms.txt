# Varda Space Industries

> Varda is a microgravity-enabled life sciences company that processes materials in orbit and returns them to Earth. Its W-Series spacecraft are free-flying orbital production satellites with reentry capability, used for in-orbit pharmaceutical processing and as a hypersonic reentry testbed for government flight testing.
